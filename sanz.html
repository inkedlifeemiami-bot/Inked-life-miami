Sanz

Born and raised in Cuba, Sanz has cultivated his artistry into mastery, becoming one of Miami’s top micro realism tattoo artists. With an extraordinary eye for detail and a passion for transforming skin into living art, Sanz has earned a reputation for creating hyper-realistic tattoos that capture intricate textures, lifelike depth, and breathtaking precision.

His dedication to the craft, combined with years of experience, has made him a sought-after artist among collectors and enthusiasts who appreciate fine artistry in ink. Whether it’s delicate portraits, stunning nature-inspired pieces, or intricate custom designs, Sanz brings every tattoo to life with an unmatched level of skill and artistry.

For those seeking world-class micro realism, Sanz is a name that stands out in Miami’s tattoo scene—where passion, precision, and creativity collide.
