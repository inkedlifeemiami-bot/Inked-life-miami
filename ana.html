Madeline

Madeline is an American tattoo artist born and raised in Texas, who has been honing her craft since 2013. Alongside tattooing, she also has experience in painting and illustration. Throughout her career, she has worked in several studios across Texas, where she has learned from other artists and continued to develop her skills.

Growing up surrounded by the diverse landscapes of the Lone Star State, her work is deeply influenced by her Southern roots. She is inspired by nature, including local botanicals, sprawling landscapes, and native fauna, which she often incorporates into her tattoo designs.

As an artist, Madi enjoys exploring different styles and techniques to create unique and personalized tattoos for her clients. She believes that each piece should be special and reflective of the individuality of the person wearing it. Working closely with clients to ensure their vision is brought to life, she takes pride in creating tattoos that hold deep meaning and significance.

In addition to tattooing, she is also passionate about other mediums of art, including painting and illustration. This experience has allowed her to develop an eye for detail and a keen sense of color and composition, which is evident in her custom designs.

Overall, Madi is a talented and passionate artist who draws inspiration from her Texan upbringing and the natural world. Her commitment to creating personalized and meaningful artwork has made her a sought-after artist in Texas and beyond.
