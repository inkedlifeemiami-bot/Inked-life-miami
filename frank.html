Frank

Frank is a highly skilled artist who specializes in creating black and grey realistic tattoo designs, with his own unique touch in every project he starts. This means that he has the ability to bring his own creative vision to each piece he creates, adding a personal flair that makes his work stand out from others in the industry.

When it comes to the execution process, Frank is most passionate about working on larger pieces such as sleeves and leg sleeves. These types of projects require a lot of both patience and attention to detail to ensure that the final result is both cohesive and visually stunning. However, despite his preference for larger projects, Frank is still capable of creating smaller pieces in any style that his clients may desire.

In addition to his technical skills, Frank is also known for his ability to communicate effectively with his clients. He takes the time to listen to their ideas, understand their preferences, and work with them to create a tattoo design that they will love and cherish for years to come. With his talent, experience, and dedication to his craft, it's no wonder that Frank has built a reputation as one of the top tattoo artists in his field.
