Zoey

Zoey Dupont is a tattoo artist whose signature style acts as a living bridge between the bold grit of classic Americana and the refined elegance of French illustration.

Her journey began in the fast-paced, historic street shops of the United States, where she mastered the unapologetic fundamentals of traditional tattooing: pulling unbreakable lines and packing solid pigment. Seeking to elevate her craft, Zoey then relocated to the intimate private studios of Paris. There, she absorbed the meticulous detail, fine-line stippling, and delicate shading that define modern European tattooing.

Today, Zoey's custom work perfectly balances these two distinct histories. She seamlessly merges the stark, heavy impact of traditional American motifs with the soft, intricate aesthetics of French fine art, creating deeply personal pieces that deliver raw edge with meticulous elegance
